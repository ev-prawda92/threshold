The full ask

Base
$270,000floor $250,000

86th percentile of their own posted range. Never state this first — if they ask for a number before making an offer, give the target, never a history.

Sign-on
Ask $40,000expect $25–30,000

Ask above what you will accept, because you will not get more than you name. This is the most gettable line in the package: it comes from a different budget than base, it does not disturb the level band, and it is a one-time number a recruiter can approve without a comp review.

Equity
Ask $300K / 4 yrs~$75K per year · settle $200–250K

State the grant in dollars, never share count. Aurora’s own filed offer letters convert a dollar value into units using a 20-trading-day average price, so the share count is set near the grant date and a number agreed in shares can quietly change value. Also ask for the annual refresh policy in writing, and — if base is capped by the band — for the shortfall to move here rather than disappear.

Bonus
~15% targetconfirm the number, don’t negotiate it

Aurora’s SEC-filed executive letters carry a 40% target; individual-contributor levels run far lower, and 10–20% is the realistic band. It is policy, not a lever — but you need the figure to compare offers, and a prorated first year is part of what the sign-on covers.

Total
$355–380Kthe number that actually matters

$270K base + ~$40K bonus + $50–75K annualized equity. Verified Product Manager packages at Aurora run from a $272K median to $379K at the top; Senior Staff belongs at the upper end of that. Program Manager packages sit far lower ($157–229K) — make sure the conversation is anchored on the product ladder, since this req sits in Product Management & Design.

Why the sign-on, specifically

Aurora’s filed offer letters vest RSUs 18.75% at a nine-month cliff, then 1/16 quarterly — not the twelve-month cliff most companies use. So the equity gap is three quarters of a year, and then a chunk lands at once. That makes the sign-on argument narrower and more precise: it bridges nine months of no equity plus a prorated first-year bonus, while billable revenue stops on day one. Rule that generalizes: read the vesting schedule before sizing the sign-on — the cliff length is the ask, and it is not always twelve months.

Vesting terms and the 20-day pricing mechanism come from an Aurora employment letter filed as a 10-K exhibit. It is an executive letter, so treat the structure as Aurora’s standard and the magnitudes as not comparable.SEC · AUR 10-K exhibit 10.30

Sequence

  1. Do not anchor first. “I’d like to understand the full package before naming a number” is a complete answer to the first comp question.
  2. If pressed, use the rate, not the income. “I’m independent at $125 an hour. Annualized that’s about $260K gross, before employer taxes, benefits, and unbilled time. I’m targeting $270K base.” True, and it anchors at the top of their band.
  3. Negotiate the package, not the base. Bands are policy and recruiters cannot break them; sign-on and equity are where the discretion lives.
  4. Get the whole offer before countering once. One considered counter across every component beats three rounds on salary alone.
