Below the four-fifths threshold. Selection rate for candidates with caregiving indicators is 71% of the highest group’s — the pattern an adverse-impact review looks for, and the kind of finding that has to be disclosed under an annual bias audit. It is also unnecessary as a screen: travel is a condition of the job, not a qualification. Confirm it at offer stage and it stops filtering the pipeline while remaining a requirement of the role.

Three of your five successful hires did not state both words, and two were hired anyway after a hiring-manager override. A gate your own team routinely overrides is not a gate — it is a keyword tax on candidates who describe the same work differently.

A real constraint, but it is the last bullet in the posting. Nine candidates withdrew after a screen once they learned of it — roughly 40 hours of recruiter time spent on people the first paragraph could have filtered.

The other seven requirements screen nobody. “Strong written and verbal communication,” “solid technical aptitude,” and five more are stated under Required Qualifications and have never been the recorded reason for a rejection. They are not harmful, but the posting reads as eleven demands when four are real — and length correlates with self-selection out, particularly among candidates who match on substance and not on vocabulary.
