Would you click one of these?

Threshold reads a job posting and splits it into hard gates — the requirements that actually disqualify someone — and everything else. When you reject a candidate, it offers that req’s own gates instead of a generic dropdown, pre-checked from what it found in the résumé.

Below is a real posting and thirteen candidates. Reject a few the way you normally would. Two minutes. It is testing one thing: whether picking a specific reason is workable at the moment you actually make the call.
